Every Pixel Has a Story.

PixelProof AI is an advanced forensic research tool designed to analyze images and classify them based on AI generation traces versus genuine photographs.

Local ML Inference

Processing is entirely local using PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers. No data is sent to external APIs.

Pretrained Vision

Powered by a Swin Transformer tailored for image origin detection.

Supported Formats

Upload JPEG, PNG or WEBP up to 10MB.

Important Limitations

This tool provides model predictions based on statistical patterns, not definitive forensic proof. Scores indicate similarity to training data classes. Always evaluate results in context.
